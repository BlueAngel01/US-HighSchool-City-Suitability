# U.S. High School City Suitability — Research Data

## Overview

This repository contains datasets and supporting materials for a mathematical modeling study evaluating U.S. metropolitan areas from the perspective of high school students.

## Data Contents and Purpose

The data support two main research objectives:

**1. Representative City Selection**

Demographic, economic, and industrial data are used to select 10 representative metropolitan areas from 195 candidates across nine U.S. Census divisions.

**2. Student Suitability Evaluation**

Data on education, healthcare, environment, safety, transportation, living costs, and youth resources are used to evaluate and compare the selected metropolitan areas.

## Data Organization

All research materials are stored in the `data/` directory:

- `01_RAW_DATA/` — Original datasets and source documents.
- `03_PROCESSED_DATA/` — Cleaned and processed indicator data.
- `CITY_SELECTION/` — City selection data and modeling results.
- `STUDENT_SUITABILITY_DATA/` — Data for student suitability evaluation.
- Other directories — Supporting materials, historical versions, and data audits.

## Data Sources

Data are primarily obtained from publicly available sources, including the U.S. Census Bureau, BEA, NCES, EPA, FBI, NHTSA, FTA, HRSA, IMLS, and other documented organizations.

## Data Usage and Limitations

Datasets may vary in reporting year, geographic coverage, and statistical definitions. Some values are provisional or estimated.

Users should consult the original sources and consider these limitations when interpreting or reusing the data.

**Intended Use:** Academic research and educational purposes.
